1. **0–10 s — Establish the boundary.** Open `/demo?fixture=FIX-DEMO-BROKEN`. Point to the synthetic disclosure, local/native/human authority key, and exact seven-state path.
2. **10–22 s — Run the broken equivalent.** Activate **Run broken fixture**. When the permission card appears, reach **Approve synthetic log access once** in ordinary Tab order and activate it.
3. **22–40 s — Diagnose.** In review, name the three local contract findings: `episode-budget-exceeded`, `duplicate-stall-episode`, and `unexpected-focus-change`. Show their raw sequence pointers and the expected/observed timeline.
4. **40–55 s — Compare the correction.** Activate **Run fixed fixture**, approve the same synthetic tool request, and show the exact result: “Local observable checks passed — native verification required.”
5. **55–75 s — Preserve evidence.** Open the evidence preview. Show contract, fixture, provider, browser, viewport, run/attempt, integrity hash, and JSON/Markdown downloads.
6. **75–90 s — Close honestly.** Reiterate that local facts cover event order, DOM focus, controls, transcript identity/content, and mutations. Native speech, disabled-user experience, and conformance were not tested and remain authoritative future review.

Do not describe the result as accessible, screen-reader passed, WCAG passed, compliant, safe, customer-validated, or production-proven.
