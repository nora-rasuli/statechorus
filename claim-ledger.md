## Claims withheld

The following statements have no approved evidence and must not appear as positive
claims in the README, case study, repository metadata, portfolio, or social copy:

- Native screen-reader output or success for NVDA, VoiceOver, JAWS, TalkBack, or any
  other assistive technology.
- Disabled-user usability, efficiency, confidence, preference, or task success.
- WCAG conformance, certification, compliance, legal safety, or a safe release gate.
- Validated correlation between local DOM signals and native assistive-technology
  behavior.
- Prevention of inaccessible releases or defects; production effectiveness.
- Customer interviews, pilots, validation, demand, adoption, payment, revenue, ROI,
  time savings, business impact, or commercial viability.
- Model accuracy, quality, latency, or cost optimization; the product uses no model.
- Public production performance, reliability, compatibility, support, traffic, or
  availability.
- Trademark, domain, package, or repository ownership/clearance, or a durable moat.

## Recheck rule

Any public-copy change must be checked against this ledger. Any new metric needs its
date, method/profile, source, comparison basis, and limitation before publication.
Production claims require a post-deploy Stage 06 check and cannot be inferred from
the local evidence above.
